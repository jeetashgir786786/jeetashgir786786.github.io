    About

    I'm Jeetash, an electrical engineer and developer at NUST‑PNEC. Clients come to me when they need one person who can take an idea across the whole stack — the schematic, the board, the firmware on it, the dashboard that reads it, and the simulation that proves it works.

    My work spans embedded systems (STM32, ESP32, Jetson Nano), custom PCB design, power electronics, AI/ML and simulation — from fabric-defect detection to IoT-enabled industrial safety gear.

    Outside the lab I founded VisuAle, a visual content studio, and led marketing & corporate sponsorships for Formula Electric Racing NUST. That's taught me to communicate clearly, document well and keep to deadlines.

    • Manufacturing-ready files, not just prototypes
    • Clear documentation and weekly updates
    • Hardware, firmware and software in one place
    • Remote support after delivery
